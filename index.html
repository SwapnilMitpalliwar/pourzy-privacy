Pourzy – Privacy Policy

Effective date: 1 November 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game Pourzy ("the game") collects, why, and the choices you have. The game is published by Swapnil Ashok Mitpalliwar, trading as Swapie ("we", "us").

Summary

  • We do not ask for your name, email address or any account to play, and we have no accounts or servers of our own. Signing in to Google Play Games is optional.
  • Your progress and settings are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the game deletes them.
  • The game is free and shows ads. Our advertising and in-app purchase partners collect device information as described below.
  • We do not use an analytics or crash-reporting service of our own. Basic crash and performance statistics come to us from Google Play for people who installed the game from Google Play.
  • The game is for players aged 13 and over.
  • You can change your ad privacy choices at any time in Settings → Privacy choices (where available).

Information stored on your device

Your level progress, stars, hints, daily puzzle history, settings and whether you bought Remove Ads are saved locally on your device. The game also keeps a small activity log on your device (for example which level you finished and how many moves it took) to help us improve the game when you choose to share it with us. This data is not sent to us or anyone else automatically.

Your choices

  • Ad consent: where required you are asked on first launch, and you can change your choice in Settings → Privacy choices.
  • Advertising ID on Android: you can reset or delete it in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Google Play Games: sign-in is optional. You can sign out or change your profile visibility in the Play Games settings. Resetting your progress in the game also overwrites the backup.
  • Remove Ads: the optional purchase stops the ads shown between levels. Watching a video for an extra hint or tube always stays optional.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EEA/UK or the CCPA/CPRA in California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, to object to or restrict processing, and to withdraw consent. Because we do not hold an account or your progress on our own servers, most data is held by the providers above; you can exercise your rights with them directly or contact us and we will help. We do not sell personal information.

Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will help remove it.

Data retention and security

Local data stays on your device until you uninstall the game. Other providers keep data according to their own retention policies (see their privacy policies linked above). Data is transmitted over encrypted connections.

Changes

We may update this policy. The effective date above shows when it last changed. Significant changes will be noted in the game's store listing.
